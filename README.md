# American Airlines On-Time Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes American Airlines flight delays and cancellations at Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT) to identify operational patterns and opportunities to improve on-time performance.

## Data Source

U.S. Department of Transportation, Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS)

Dataset: Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance (1987–Present)

https://www.transtats.bts.gov/ontime/

**Analysis Period:** 2025
**Airline:** American Airlines (AA)
**Airport:** Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT)

## Research Objectives

**RO1: Descriptive Analysis**
Identify routes, airports, days, months, and departure periods associated with high delay and cancellation rates.

**RO2: Regression Analysis**
Identify flight and operational characteristics associated with arrival delays.

**RO3: Clustering Analysis**
Group flights or routes with similar delay patterns to recommend targeted operational improvements.
